UNDERGRADUATE THESIS · 2018 · UNPUBLISHED

Hydro-geochemical Investigation of Groundwater Quality and Human Health Risk

Noakhali Sadar Upazilla, Bangladesh · Noakhali Science and Technology University

Investigated groundwater suitability for drinking and irrigation, salinity, heavy metal contamination, and potential human health risks. The study analyzed 30 groundwater samples in duplicate, finding that 90% exceeded the permissible groundwater quality index limit and 65% of sampling locations were unsuitable for irrigation.

Health risk assessments indicated substantial potential risks for adults and children. Received the highest marks for the thesis presentation.

Study methods: GIS spatial analysis, PCA, cluster and correlation analysis, water quality and heavy metal pollution indices, ion chromatography (IC), flame atomic absorption spectrophotometry (FAAS), hazard quotient and hazard index, cancer risk assessment.

Research details

The study identified predominantly Na–Cl type groundwater and natural geogenic processes as major influences on water quality. Spatial analysis evaluated variation in groundwater quality and contamination indices. The assessment identified elevated arsenic and lead-related cancer risks.

Abstract authors: Fatema Shormin Orni, Tasrina Rabia Choudhury, and Mohammad Mahbub Kabir. Completed and presented; not published in a journal or online repository.
